## 🍪 Cookie Policy

### What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences, analyzing how you use our site, and personalizing content.

This Cookie Policy explains how CVCircle uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website and how you can control them.

### Types of Cookies We Use

#### Essential Cookies
These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation, access to secure areas, and form submissions.

- NextAuth.js session cookies (next-auth.session-token)
- Firebase authentication cookies
- CSRF protection cookies (csrf-token)
- User session management cookies
- Email verification token cookies
- Security and access control cookies

#### Performance Cookies
These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

- Google Analytics cookies
- Page load time tracking
- Error tracking and monitoring
- User behavior analysis

#### Functional Cookies
These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences and settings.

- Theme preferences (dark/light mode)
- CV template and design preferences
- User interface customization settings
- Dashboard layout preferences
- Notification preferences
- Onboarding completion status

#### Marketing Cookies
These cookies are used to track visitors across websites to display relevant and engaging advertisements.

- Social media integration cookies
- Advertising network cookies
- Retargeting cookies
- Conversion tracking cookies

### Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services that may place cookies on your device:

- **Google Analytics**: To understand how visitors use our website
- **Payment Processors**: Stripe, Razorpay for secure payment processing
- **Social Media**: When you interact with social media features on our website

### Cookie Duration

#### Session Cookies
These cookies are temporary and are deleted when you close your browser. They are used to maintain your session while you browse our website.

#### Persistent Cookies
These cookies remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them. They help us remember your preferences and provide personalized experiences.

### Managing Your Cookie Preferences

#### Browser Settings
You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:
- View and delete existing cookies
- Block cookies from specific websites
- Block all cookies
- Set preferences for different types of cookies

#### Cookie Consent
When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:
- Accept all cookies
- Customize your cookie preferences
- Reject non-essential cookies
- Learn more about our cookie usage

#### Opt-Out Options
- Google Analytics: Use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
- Marketing cookies: Use the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out tool
- Social media: Adjust your privacy settings on social media platforms

### Impact of Disabling Cookies

While you can disable cookies, doing so may affect your experience:
- You may not be able to log in to your account
- Some features may not work properly
- Your preferences may not be saved
- Security features may be compromised

## 💾 Data Saving & Storage

### Data Storage Locations

#### Primary Database: MongoDB
- **Location**: MongoDB Atlas (cloud-hosted)
- **Data Stored**: 
  - User accounts and profiles
  - CV content and cover letters
  - Job applications and tracking data
  - Journey and analytics data
  - Templates and settings
- **Encryption**: Data encrypted at rest and in transit
- **Backup**: Regular automated backups
- **Retention**: Data retained as long as account is active

#### File Storage: AWS S3
- **Location**: Amazon Web Services S3
- **Data Stored**:
  - CV templates
  - User-uploaded files
  - Generated PDFs and documents
  - Profile images
- **Access Control**: Private buckets with secure access
- **Encryption**: Server-side encryption enabled

#### Session Storage: NextAuth.js
- **Location**: HTTP-only cookies
- **Data Stored**:
  - JWT tokens
  - Session identifiers
  - User authentication state
- **Security**: Secure, HTTP-only, SameSite cookies
- **Expiration**: Configurable session timeout

### Data Saving Mechanisms

#### Auto-Save Functionality
- **CV Studio**: Auto-saves every 30 seconds during editing
- **Form Data**: Auto-saves on field blur
- **Draft System**: Temporary drafts saved to session storage
- **Recovery**: Automatic recovery of unsaved changes

#### Manual Save
- **Save Button**: Explicit save action in CV Studio
- **Export**: Saves exported documents to user's device
- **Download**: Downloads CVs in various formats

#### Data Backup
- **Database Backups**: Daily automated backups
- **Retention Period**: 30 days of backup retention
- **Recovery**: Point-in-time recovery available
- **Testing**: Regular backup restoration testing

### Data Retention Policy

#### Active Accounts
- **Retention**: Data retained as long as account is active
- **Inactivity**: Accounts inactive for 2+ years may be archived
- **Notification**: Users notified before account archival

#### Deleted Accounts
- **Deletion**: Data deleted within 30 days of account deletion
- **Backup Retention**: Backups may retain data for up to 90 days
- **Legal Compliance**: Some data may be retained for legal requirements

#### CV Data
- **Master CV**: Retained as long as account is active
- **Tailored CVs**: Retained with associated job applications
- **Deleted CVs**: Permanently deleted after 30 days

#### Job Application Data
- **Active Applications**: Retained indefinitely
- **Completed Applications**: Retained for 2 years
- **Archived Applications**: Retained for 5 years

## 🔐 Data Security

### Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

#### Encryption
- **Data in Transit**: TLS/SSL encryption for all data transmission
- **Data at Rest**: Encryption of database and file storage
- **Encryption Standards**: AES-256 encryption

#### Authentication Security
- **NextAuth.js**: Secure authentication with JWT tokens
- **Firebase**: Additional authentication security
- **Password Security**: Hashed passwords (bcrypt)
- **Session Management**: Secure HTTP-only cookies

#### Access Controls
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: User roles and permissions
- **API Security**: Authentication required for all API endpoints
- **Admin Access**: Restricted admin access with audit logging

#### Network Security
- **Firewall Protection**: Network-level security
- **DDoS Protection**: Protection against distributed denial-of-service attacks
- **Intrusion Detection**: Monitoring for unauthorized access

#### Regular Security Audits
- **Vulnerability Assessments**: Regular security scans
- **Penetration Testing**: Periodic penetration tests
- **Code Reviews**: Security-focused code reviews
- **Dependency Updates**: Regular security updates

### Incident Response

In the event of a data breach:
1. **Detection**: Immediate detection and assessment
2. **Containment**: Contain the breach to prevent further damage
3. **Notification**: Notify affected users within 72 hours (if required)
4. **Investigation**: Investigate the cause and extent
5. **Remediation**: Fix vulnerabilities and prevent recurrence
6. **Documentation**: Document the incident and response
