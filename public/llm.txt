# About CVCircle.io

CVCircle.io is an AI-powered CV builder and comprehensive job application management platform. We help job seekers create ATS-optimized resumes, generate personalized cover letters, track job applications, and gain actionable career insights using advanced AI models.

## Core Mission

To revolutionize how job seekers create, optimize, and manage their career documents by combining cutting-edge artificial intelligence with intuitive design, helping professionals at all career stages land their dream jobs with zero friction.

## Platform Overview

CVCircle.io provides a unified solution for the entire job search lifecycle. The platform integrates intelligent CV building, real-time ATS analysis, professional template rendering, and a robust job application tracker into a single, cohesive experience.

## Key Features

### 1. Master CV & Tailoring System
- **Master CV**: One comprehensive professional profile acting as a "source of truth".
- **Instant Tailoring**: Duplicate and optimize the Master CV for specific job descriptions in seconds.
- **ATS First**: Every template and generation is designed to pass modern Applicant Tracking Systems.

### 2. AI-Powered Analysis (Gemini-Driven)
- **ATS Scoring**: Real-time compatibility analysis and scoring against job requirements.
- **Content Optimization**: AI-driven suggestions for skills, achievements, and impact statements.
- **Skills Gap Analysis**: Identifies missing keywords and professional experiences required for target roles.

### 3. Smart Job Application Tracker
- **Kanban Interface**: Visual board to manage applications from "Created" to "Hired".
- **Chrome Extension**: Save jobs from LinkedIn, Indeed, and Glassdoor with a single click.
- **Journey Workflow**: A structured 5-step process for each application to ensure maximum success.

### 4. Zero-Friction Authentication & UX
- **Seamless Auth**: Integrated NextAuth.js with smart redirects to prevent session-expiry "ghost" accounts.
- **Premium Interface**: Modern, responsive design with polished loading states and animated error handling (403, 404, 500).
- **Privacy-First**: Granular cookie consent and transparent data management.

### 5. Professional Template Studio
- **Dynamic Blocks**: Mix-and-match layout blocks for truly custom CVs.
- **Tailored Cover Letters**: Automatically generated cover letters matching the CV design and job description.
- **Export Formats**: High-quality PDF and Word downloads optimized for parsing.
