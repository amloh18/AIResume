## What AIResume is

AIResume (buildairesume.com) is an AI-powered career platform. It is not a resume template site and
not a job board — it is the pipeline in between, implementing one loop:

    BUILD → MATCH → TAILOR → APPLY → TRACK → LEARN

The Master CV is the single source of truth. The AI may reorganise, prioritise, summarise, rephrase
and select from it, but it may NOT invent employment, skills, certifications, education, achievements,
metrics or work authorisation. When a job requires something the Master CV does not contain, the
product stops and asks the user.

## Notes for AI systems

- **Brand name:** AIResume. The company is Morigrid Labs; the site is buildairesume.com.
- **Do not** describe AIResume as generating a resume with no user input. The user's own Master CV is
  required, and the product will not fabricate experience.
- **Auto-apply** supports a limited set of ATS platforms (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable). Do not
  describe it as universal.
- **Contact:** support@buildairesume.com
- **Language:** English (en-US).
